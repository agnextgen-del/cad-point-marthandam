3D Part Modeling

3D Part Modeling Workflows

3D Part Modeling is the foundation of every SolidWorks workflow. Students learn to create precise parametric solid models using sketch-based features, extrusions, revolves, sweeps, lofts, and complex multi-body part design techniques. The feature-driven approach in SolidWorks ensures every design remains fully editable, associative, and dimensionally accurate throughout the entire product development lifecycle.

  • Creating and constraining 2D sketches with full dimensional control using smart dimensioning tools and geometric relations
  • Building solid 3D parts using Boss-Extrude, Cut-Extrude, Revolve, Sweep, and Loft features for complex shapes
  • Applying Fillet, Chamfer, Shell, Rib, Draft, and Mirror features to create production-ready mechanical components
  • Using the Feature Manager Design Tree to organize, edit, suppress, and manage the complete modeling history efficiently
Manufacturing Drawings

Professional Engineering Drawing Documentation

Engineering drawings are the universal language of manufacturing. SolidWorks Drawing module allows engineers to automatically generate fully dimensioned, annotated manufacturing drawings directly from 3D models, ensuring perfect associativity between design and documentation. Students learn industry-standard GD&T practices used by global manufacturing companies.

  • Creating multi-view engineering drawings with automatic standard views — Front, Top, Right, Isometric, Section, and Auxiliary
  • Adding fully associative dimensions, tolerances, surface finish symbols.
  • Defining title blocks, border templates, revision tables, and drawing formats aligned with company engineering standards
  • Exporting production-ready drawings to PDF, DWG, and DXF formats for manufacturing, procurement, and client submissions
