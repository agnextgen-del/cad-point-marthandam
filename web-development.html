No prior programming experience is required for our beginner batches. We start from absolute zero — covering computer basics, problem-solving thinking, and gradually building up to advanced concepts. Students from any educational background can enroll and succeed in our programs.
Which course should I choose — Java, Python, or Full Stack?
It depends on your career goals. Java is ideal for enterprise and banking sector jobs. Python is best for those interested in web development with AI/ML integration. Full Stack is the most versatile and offers the widest range of job opportunities. We offer free counseling sessions to help you decide the best path based on your background and goals.

Each batch has a maximum of 15 students to ensure personalized attention. Classes are conducted in fully equipped computer labs. Both offline (at our centre) and online (via Zoom) options are available. All sessions are recorded for later review.

Our dedicated placement cell begins preparing you from Week 6 of your course. We also organize on-campus recruitment drives every quarter.

Yes! We offer flexible payment plans including 2–6 month EMI options with zero interest. We also have early-bird discounts for upfront payments and special pricing for students, referrals, and group enrollments. Contact our admissions team to discuss the best plan for your budget.
