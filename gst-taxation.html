GST Filing Systems

GST Filing & Management

Master India's GST portal workflow from registration to quarterly and monthly return filing. This module covers GSTIN registration, invoice management under GST, input tax credit (ITC) reconciliation, e-way bill generation, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9 filing, and handling GST notices.

01
GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B Monthly Return Filing

Learn to file outward supply returns (GSTR-1) and summary returns (GSTR-3B) accurately — including adjustments, amendments, and nil returns for small businesses.

02
Input Tax Credit (ITC) Reconciliation

Understand ITC eligibility rules, GSTR-2A/2B reconciliation with purchase registers, and reversal provisions to ensure maximum tax benefit for businesses.

03
E-Way Bill & Invoice Management

Master e-way bill generation, invoice formats under GST, debit/credit note procedures, and reverse charge mechanism applicable to various business transactions.

Course Curriculum

Complete GST & Taxation Course Modules

A structured, industry-aligned curriculum covering every aspect of GST, taxation, payroll, accounting, and financial compliance.

Module 01
GST Fundamentals

Concept of GST, types (CGST/SGST/IGST/UTGST), GST registration, composition scheme, taxable supply, exempt supply, and GST council structure.

Module 02
GST Return Filing

GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-4, GSTR-9, GSTR-9C filing on the GST portal — monthly, quarterly, and annual return workflows with late fees and penalties.

Module 03
Income Tax Concepts

Income heads, assessment year vs financial year, residential status, taxable income computation, deductions, exemptions, and advance tax provisions.

Module 04
ITR Filing Systems

Practical ITR-1 to ITR-4 filing for salaried employees, professionals, and small businesses on the Income Tax portal with all schedules and declarations.

Module 05
TDS & TCS Management

TDS provisions under key sections, TDS deduction and deposit, 24Q/26Q return filing, Form 16/16A generation, and lower deduction certificate procedures.

Module 06
Payroll Management

Salary structure design, CTC computation, PF/ESIC/PT deductions, payslip generation, Form 16 preparation, and full & final settlement processing.

Module 07
Financial Accounting

Double-entry bookkeeping, journal entries, ledger posting, trial balance, Trading & P&L Account, Balance Sheet, and depreciation accounting.

Module 08
Invoice Management

GST invoice formats, credit/debit notes, reverse charge mechanism, e-invoice generation, e-way bill procedures, and invoice reconciliation techniques.

Module 09
Auditing Basics

Audit concepts, types of audits, statutory vs internal audit, vouching & verification techniques, audit trail, CARO requirements, and audit report structure.

Module 10
Tally Prime Integration

Tally Prime configuration for GST, ledger groups, voucher entries, bank reconciliation, reports generation, inventory management, and payroll in Tally.

Module 11
Business Compliance

ROC filing basics, MCA portal, Companies Act compliance, annual return filing, statutory registers, GST audit, and business compliance checklist management.

Module 12
Real-Time Projects

Work on live business scenarios — filing GSTR-1 for a manufacturing company, preparing payroll for 50+ employees, and building complete financial statements from scratch.
