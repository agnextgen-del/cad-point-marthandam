PLC Integration & Industrial Automation

EPLAN's PLC integration capabilities allow engineers to import PLC I/O data, automatically generate connection diagrams, and maintain synchronization between PLC programming and electrical documentation. This feature is critical for modern automation projects where hundreds of I/O points must be accurately documented, eliminating manual errors and reducing project timelines significantly.

  • PLC I/O Planning: Import and manage PLC data from Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi, and other major brands.
  • Automatic I/O Diagrams: Generate I/O wiring diagrams automatically from PLC configuration data.
  • Fieldbus & Network Design: Document PROFIBUS, PROFINET, EtherNet/IP, and DeviceNet communication networks.
  • Safety Circuit Design: Design SIL-compliant safety circuits and document functional safety systems per IEC 62061.

Standards

International Electrical Standards Covered

Understanding and applying international electrical documentation standards is what separates a professional engineer from a basic designer. Our EPLAN course covers the most critical global standards required by multinational companies, ensuring your documentation is accepted and compliant across markets from India to Germany, USA to Japan.

IEC 61082 — Electrical Documentation

The international standard for preparation of documents used in electrotechnology, covering drawing principles and symbol usage.

DIN EN 81346 — Reference Designation System

The German industrial standard for structuring and designating technical objects in electrical documentation used across Europe.

NFPA 79 — Electrical Standard for Industrial Machinery

The US standard governing the electrical equipment of industrial machinery, mandatory for any machinery exported to or used in North America.

IEC 62061 — Functional Safety

Safety-instrumented system documentation standards for designing and documenting SIL-rated safety circuits in industrial machinery.
