Core Concept

Finite Element Analysis (FEA)

Finite Element Analysis is the computational backbone of modern engineering design. FEA divides complex structures into thousands of small finite elements and applies mathematical equations to predict how those structures behave under various loading conditions, temperatures, and constraints. In this course, you will master FEA theory and apply it practically using ANSYS Workbench.

Mesh Generation & Refinement

Learn global and local mesh controls, mesh sizing, element quality checks, and advanced meshing strategies for complex CAD geometry in ANSYS.

Boundary Conditions & Loads

Apply forces, pressures, moments, fixed supports, displacement constraints, and contact definitions to accurately model real engineering scenarios.

Solver Setup & Result Analysis

Configure ANSYS solvers, monitor convergence, extract Von Mises stress, deformation, strain energy, and safety factor contour plots for engineering decisions.

Analysis Module

Structural Analysis

Structural Analysis in ANSYS enables engineers to predict how mechanical components and assemblies respond to real-world forces, pressures, and constraints. This critical module covers both static and dynamic analysis techniques used daily in automotive component validation, machinery design, civil structural checks, and aerospace load analysis.

Static Structural Analysis

Determine stress distributions, deflections, and safety factors for components under static loads. Master result probing, path operations, and design validation workflows.

Modal & Dynamic Analysis

Compute natural frequencies, mode shapes, and harmonic responses. Understand resonance phenomena critical for rotating machinery, bridges, and vibrating structures.

Contact & Assembly Simulation

Configure bonded, frictional, and frictionless contact pairs in multi-body assemblies. Simulate bolt pre-tension, interference fits, and joint behaviour under loading.

Curriculum

Course Modules

A carefully structured, industry-curated curriculum covering all aspects of ANSYS simulation — from fundamentals to advanced real-world engineering analysis.

01
Introduction to ANSYS

ANSYS product family, Workbench interface navigation, project schematic setup, geometry import from SolidWorks/CATIA, and basic simulation workflow.

02
FEA Fundamentals

Stiffness matrix formulation, element types (1D/2D/3D), shape functions, numerical integration, global assembly, convergence theory, and accuracy.

03
Meshing Techniques

Automatic and manual meshing, sweep mesh, hex-dominant mesh, inflation layers, mesh quality metrics (skewness, orthogonality), and local refinement strategies.

04
Static Structural Analysis

Boundary conditions, load application, material assignment, nonlinear analysis, contact definitions, result extraction and evaluation including Von Mises stress and deformation.

05
Dynamic Analysis

Modal analysis, harmonic response, transient structural analysis, random vibration, spectrum analysis, and frequency response function generation for real engineering components.

06
Thermal Analysis

Steady-state and transient thermal analysis, heat generation, convection, radiation, thermal-structural coupling, and temperature-dependent material properties.

07
CFD Basics (ANSYS Fluent)

Fluid domain setup, boundary conditions (inlet/outlet/wall), turbulence model selection, solver settings, convergence monitoring, and post-processing of flow results.

08
Boundary Conditions

Force, pressure, moment, fixed support, displacement, remote force, bearing load, bolt pre-tension, spring, and joint boundary conditions with practical examples.

09
Result Interpretation

Extracting and interpreting Von Mises stress, principal stresses, equivalent strain, safety factors, deformation, reaction forces, and creating professional simulation reports.

10
Product Validation

Design optimization, topology optimization, weight reduction studies, fatigue life analysis, and the complete engineering sign-off workflow using ANSYS results.

11
Engineering Simulation Workflow

End-to-end simulation workflow from design brief to validated results. Industry best practices, version control, quality checks, and simulation governance protocols.

12
Real-Time Industry Projects

Apply all skills on 5+ real-world ANSYS projects including automotive bracket FEA, heat sink thermal analysis, pipe flow CFD, modal analysis of a machine frame, and product fatigue study.
