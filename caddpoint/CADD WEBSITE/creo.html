3D Product Modeling

Precision Parametric Modeling for Real-World Engineering

Learn to build intelligent, history-based 3D models that reflect engineering design intent. Creo's parametric engine ensures every model update propagates automatically across drawings and assemblies, saving hours of rework and preventing costly errors in manufacturing.

Feature-Based Modeling

Create complex geometry using extrude, revolve, sweep, blend, and helical features with full parametric control.

Design Intent Capture

Define relations, parameters, and family tables to create smart models that adapt to design changes automatically.

Industrial Product Development

Apply professional modeling workflows used in automotive, aerospace, and consumer electronics product development.

Complex Assembly Design with Motion Simulation
Assembly Design

Multi-Level Assembly Workflows for Complex Products

Master both bottom-up and top-down assembly methodologies to build multi-component product assemblies with precise mating constraints. Perform interference detection, clearance analysis, and create professional exploded views for documentation and presentations.

Mating Constraints System

Apply coincident, distance, angle, parallel, and tangent constraints to precisely position components in an assembly.

Interference & Clearance Analysis

Detect collisions and verify minimum clearances between moving components before sending designs for manufacturing.

Exploded Views & BOM

Create professional exploded assembly views and Bill of Materials tables for technical documentation and service manuals.
