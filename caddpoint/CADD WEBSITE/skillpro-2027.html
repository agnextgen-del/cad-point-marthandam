About the event

SKILL PRO 2K27 is CADPOINT's scholarship campaign. It is designed to identify and encourage students who want to develop industry-relevant skills but may face difficulty paying course fees.

Through SKILL PRO 2K27, CADPOINT aims to recognise student talent, reduce financial barriers to technical education and create opportunities for skill development. The campaign is not just about conducting a scholarship examination; it is about motivating students to learn new skills, build confidence and prepare themselves for future career opportunities.

Main objectives

  • Support deserving students through course-fee scholarships.
  • Increase awareness of CADPOINT courses and career-oriented training.
  • Reach more schools, colleges and student communities.
  • Generate qualified enquiries and admissions through transparent personal counselling.
  • Build SKILL PRO into a recognizable annual CADPOINT initiative.
