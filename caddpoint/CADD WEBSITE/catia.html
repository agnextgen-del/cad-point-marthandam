Course Overview

CATIA V5/V6 Course

CATIA (Computer Aided Three-dimensional Interactive Application) is a world-class multi-platform software suite developed by Dassault Systèmes. It is the industry standard for product design and development in automotive, aerospace, industrial machinery, and consumer goods manufacturing sectors worldwide. Mastering CATIA V5/V6 opens doors to high-paying engineering roles in global MNCs.

  • Industry-standard parametric solid and surface modeling environment trusted by Fortune 500 companies
  • Complete product lifecycle management from concept design to manufacturing drawings
  • Advanced assembly design, constraints management, and digital mockup capabilities
  • Integrated simulation, kinematics, and stress analysis modules for robust product validation

Assembly Design

Mastering Product Assembly Workflows

CATIA's Assembly Design workbench allows engineers to create complex multi-component product assemblies with precision mechanical constraints, motion simulation, and interference detection — essential for validating product designs before physical prototyping.

  • Mechanical Constraints SystemCoincident, offset, contact, angular, and fix constraints for accurate part positioning.
  • Digital Mockup (DMU)Virtual product validation through DMU Navigator, Kinematics, and Fitting simulation.
  • Clash & Clearance AnalysisAutomatic interference detection and clearance measurement between assembly components.
  • BOM & Properties ManagementAutomated Bill of Materials generation and component properties for manufacturing documentation.
