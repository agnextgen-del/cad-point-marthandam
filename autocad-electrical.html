In AutoCAD Electrical, you learn to create industry-standard schematic diagrams using built-in IEC and NEMA symbol libraries, enabling precise representation of circuits, components, and interconnections for industrial, commercial, and infrastructure projects.

Single-Line & Multi-Line Diagrams

Design both single-line overview diagrams and detailed multi-line schematic drawings that clearly communicate electrical systems to engineers and technicians.

Designing PLC panels and control cabinets is one of the most in-demand skills in the electrical engineering and industrial automation industry. AutoCAD Electrical provides a complete environment for creating panel layout drawings, enclosure designs, component placement diagrams, and full wiring documentation packages that manufacturing companies demand from qualified electrical design engineers.

Create accurate panel enclosure layouts showing DIN rail mounting, component spacing, cable tray routing, and ventilation requirements for industrial control panels.

Terminal Block & Wiring Schedules

Generate terminal block diagrams, wire routing schedules, interconnection drawings, and complete panel wiring documentation automatically from schematic data.

Bill of Materials Generation

Automatically extract and generate detailed bills of materials, component lists, cable schedules, and procurement specifications directly from your panel design drawings.

Design Workflow

Electrical Design Workflow Process

A systematic, industry-proven workflow for delivering complete electrical design packages from concept to documentation

Requirements Analysis

Analyze project scope, electrical load requirements, applicable standards, and client specifications to establish the design basis and drawing package deliverables.

Schematic Creation

Develop electrical schematics using AutoCAD Electrical tools — placing symbols, wiring circuits, assigning component tags, and establishing cross-references across drawing sheets.

Panel Layout Design

Create physical panel layout drawings with accurate component placement, DIN rail allocation, cable entry, and space planning based on the completed schematic drawings.

Design Verification

Run AutoCAD Electrical's built-in error checking tools, verify cross-references, validate wire numbering consistency, and ensure compliance with applicable electrical standards.

Documentation Generation

Auto-generate bill of materials, wire schedules, terminal block reports, cable schedules, and all project documentation reports directly from the drawing data.

Circuit Analysis

Perform electrical circuit analysis, verify load calculations, check cable sizing adequacy, and review protection coordination as part of the complete design review process.

Design Revision Control

Manage drawing revisions, maintain revision history tables, issue revision clouds on updated drawings, and distribute controlled copies of electrical design packages to stakeholders.

Deliverable Submission

Package complete drawing sets for client submission, generate PDF drawing packages, export data to Excel for procurement, and prepare as-built documentation for handover.
