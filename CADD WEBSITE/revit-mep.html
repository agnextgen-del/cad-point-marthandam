Cable Tray Systems

Cable Tray Routing & Containment Design

Cable tray routing is a fundamental skill for any MEP electrical engineer working on modern building projects. This module teaches you to design, model, and coordinate complete cable tray systems within the BIM environment, from initial pathway planning and load calculations to 3D routing, bend coordination, and support structure design.

  • Pathway Planning & Sizing
    Calculate cable fill ratios, select appropriate tray sizes, plan optimal routing paths through buildings while avoiding clashes with structural elements and other MEP systems.
  • 3D Tray Routing in Revit MEP
    Model horizontal runs, vertical risers, bends, tees, crosses, reducers, and all cable containment fittings accurately in 3D with automatic parameter-driven dimensions.
  • Support Structure Design
    Design and place cable tray support hangers, cantilever brackets, and trapeze supports with proper spacing according to manufacturer specifications and load ratings.
  • Material Takeoff & Scheduling
    Generate automated cable tray material schedules, length takeoffs, and procurement lists directly from the Revit MEP model to support accurate project estimation.

Clash Detection & BIM Coordination

One of the most powerful advantages of BIM over traditional CAD is the ability to detect and resolve design conflicts before construction begins. This module covers the complete clash detection workflow using Navisworks Manage integrated with Revit MEP, teaching you to identify spatial conflicts between electrical systems and other building disciplines, categorize clash types, run tolerance-based analysis, and produce structured clash reports for coordination meetings with project stakeholders.

  • Navisworks Clash Testing
    Run hard clash, soft clash, and workflow clash tests between electrical models and architectural, structural, HVAC, and plumbing systems to identify all potential conflicts.
  • Clash Report Generation
    Generate professional clash reports with screenshots, clash group classification, priority assignment, and responsible party identification for coordination resolution tracking.
  • BIM Coordination Meetings
    Learn the professional workflow for multi-discipline BIM coordination meetings, including model federation, 3D viewing, clash resolution documentation, and re-test verification.
  • Model Quality Control
    Implement BIM quality control processes including model checking, information validation, compliance verification, and deliverable review against BIM execution plan requirements.
