Course Curriculum

What You'll Learn

A structured, project-based curriculum designed to take you from SketchUp beginner to professional 3D designer in the shortest time possible.

01

SketchUp Interface & Navigation

Master the SketchUp workspace, toolbars, drawing axes, orbit/pan/zoom controls, and efficient 3D navigation techniques used by professionals daily.

02

3D Modeling Fundamentals

Create precise 3D geometry using push/pull, follow me, intersect faces, and advanced solid tools for complex architectural and product forms.

03

Architectural & Interior Design

Design complete building exteriors, interior spaces, furniture layouts, and space planning using real-world scale, components, and dynamic blocks.

04

Landscape & Site Design

Model terrain, pathways, planting layouts, site furniture, and outdoor spaces using SketchUp's terrain sculpting and sandbox tools.

05

Materials, Textures & Styles

Apply realistic materials, custom textures, and visual styles. Configure scenes, shadows, section cuts, and atmospheric effects for compelling presentations.

06

Rendering & Presentation

Export photorealistic renders using V-Ray for SketchUp. Create animated walkthroughs, Layout sheet presentations, and client-ready deliverables.
