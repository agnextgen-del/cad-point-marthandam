Course Modules

01
Spoken English Basics

Core grammar rules — tenses, articles, prepositions, subject-verb agreement, and sentence structures explained through practical conversation examples and exercises.

03
Vocabulary Development

Systematic vocabulary expansion using word families, synonyms, antonyms, collocations, idioms, and domain-specific professional vocabulary for daily and business use.

04
Pronunciation Training

Phonetics, stress, intonation, rhythm, and accent neutralization — guided exercises to improve clarity, natural flow, and confidence in English pronunciation and articulation.

07
Writing Skills

Public Speaking
