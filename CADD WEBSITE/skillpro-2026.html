About

About Skill Pro 2026

Skill Pro 2K26 is an event by CADPOINT Authorized Training Centre, together with AG NextGen Technologies, that recognises top-performing engineering students. The “SkillPro Toppers 2K26” are the three students who finished first, second and third.

Each topper received a cash prize, a prize cheque, a certificate and a trophy. The toppers study Electrical and Electronics Engineering and Civil Engineering at three different colleges.
