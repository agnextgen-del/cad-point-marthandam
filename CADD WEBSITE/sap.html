ERP Workflow

Understanding the SAP ERP Workflow

SAP integrates all key business processes into a single unified platform, enabling seamless data flow across departments and enabling real-time business intelligence.

01
Business Process Integration

SAP connects Finance, Procurement, Sales, HR, and Manufacturing departments in a single integrated system, eliminating data silos and enabling cross-functional collaboration with real-time data sharing across all business units.

02
Master Data Management

Central repository for all enterprise data — vendors, customers, materials, employees — ensuring data consistency, accuracy, and single source of truth across all SAP modules and business operations.

03
Transactional Processing

SAP processes millions of business transactions daily including purchase orders, invoices, payroll, inventory movements, and financial postings with built-in validation rules and workflow approvals at every stage.

04
Analytics & Reporting

Real-time dashboards, financial reports, inventory analytics, and business intelligence tools built on SAP HANA's in-memory computing engine for lightning-fast data processing and executive decision support.
