About STAAD.Pro

What is STAAD Pro?
The World's #1 Structural Analysis Tool

STAAD.Pro is the industry-leading structural analysis and design software developed by Bentley Systems. Used by over 3 million engineers across 111 countries, it enables professionals to analyze and design any type of structure — from residential buildings and bridges to offshore platforms and industrial facilities.

At CADPOINT, our STAAD Pro training covers everything from basic 3D structural modeling to advanced load analysis, beam and column design, RCC and steel structure detailing, and generating professional analysis reports that meet international standards.
